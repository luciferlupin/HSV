How is vehicle scrap value calculated?

We provide a realistic, straightforward explanation of what determines your scrap price.

01

Vehicle Curb Weight

The primary baseline is determined by the total tonnage of steel and iron in the body and chassis frame.

02

Salvageable Parts

Intact mechanical components like alternators, starters, gearbox housings, and glass can add scrap value.

03

Tyres & Wheels

Vehicles with original alloy or steel wheels and rolling tyres make logistics simpler and add material value.

04

Pickup Location

Proximity to Bhiwani and accessibility for flatbed / towing assistance influences final net value.
