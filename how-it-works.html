● TRANSPARENT WORKFLOW

From old vehicle to completed paperwork.

Scrapping an old or damaged vehicle doesn't have to be complicated. Here is exactly how HSV Auto Recycling works with vehicle owners in Bhiwani and surrounding Haryana areas.

01 STEP 1 OF 4

Get a Scrap Quote

The process begins when you share basic details about your vehicle. We ask for:

  • Vehicle registration number (e.g. HR 16 XXXX)
  • Make, model, and year of manufacturing
  • Operational condition (running, non-running, or accidental)
  • Current location where vehicle is parked

Tip: Sending 2–3 photos on WhatsApp (front, rear, and odometer/engine) allows Vivek Singh to provide an accurate preliminary valuation quickly.

02 STEP 2 OF 4

Confirm Pickup & Logistics

Once quote terms are agreed upon, we coordinate the collection logistics:

  • HSV confirms pickup time and date suited to your availability.
  • Pickup assistance is coordinated for non-running or accidental vehicles.
  • The vehicle’s physical chassis number and engine number are verified against the original RC.

Vehicle owners outside Bhiwani city limits can confirm towing and pickup logistics with HSV prior to dispatch.

03 STEP 3 OF 4

Instant Payment

HSV Auto Recycling operates on the principle of direct and instant payment:

  • Payment is executed to the vehicle owner as agreed.
  • Digital bank transfers (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS) provide a permanent electronic record.
  • Payment is completed prior to or upon physical vehicle handover.
04 STEP 4 OF 4

Paperwork & RC Cancellation Support

The legal and administrative closure of the vehicle:

  • The chassis number plate is preserved according to vehicle scrapping protocols.
  • HSV provides step-by-step guidance on submitting the scrapping intimation and RC cancellation with the competent RTO.
  • Owners are advised on required document checklists to maintain clean legal standing.

Checklist for Vehicle Owners Before Handover

Please check the following before your vehicle is picked up:

✓ Remove all personal items & audio media
✓ Deactivate or peel off FASTag RFID tag
✓ Keep physical RC book / card ready
✓ Keep Aadhaar / Voter ID copy ready
