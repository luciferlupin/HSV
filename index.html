● TRANSPARENT DOCUMENTATION

Paperwork shouldn't be confusing.

We explain what is required before the vehicle moves.

Documents commonly requested may include:
  • ✓
    Original RC (Registration Certificate)
    Physical RC smart card or paper book proving vehicle ownership.
  • ✓
    Owner ID Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
    Self-attested identity proof matching the RC holder.
  • ✓
    PAN Card
    For owner verification and payment documentation.
  • ✓
    Bank Account Details / Cancelled Cheque
    For transparent digital payment to the registered vehicle owner.
  • ✓
    Other Documents (If Applicable)
    NOC from financier if financed, death certificate & succession if owner is deceased.

* Note: Document requirements vary based on vehicle status, RTO jurisdiction, and ownership type. HSV will guide you through your exact case.

OFFICIAL GUIDANCE

WHAT HAPPENS TO MY RC?

When an old or end-of-life vehicle is scrapped, the chassis number is physically cut out and preserved according to norms. HSV can guide the owner through the applicable RC cancellation and scrapping documentation process with your respective registering authority.

Why this matters:
Cancelling your RC protects you from future liabilities, misuse of the registration number, road tax liabilities, and accidental claims.
