Essential Verification Records:
  • ✓
    Original Registration Certificate (RC)
    Smart card or booklet proving legal vehicle title and technical specifications.
  • ✓
    Registered Owner KYC Proof
    Self-attested Aadhaar card or voter ID matching the VAHAN registration database.
  • ✓
    PAN Card & Account Verification
    Identity confirmation for direct, auditable electronic bank transfer.
  • ✓
    Bank NOC / Form 35 (If Hypothecated)
    Lender loan closure certificate and Form 35 confirming unencumbered ownership.
  • ✓
    Physical Chassis Number Stamping Trace
    Physical pencil tracing of the VIN plate for RTO verification file archives.
