Privacy Policy

How HSV Auto Recycling collects, uses, and safeguards information submitted through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our enquiry forms or contact us directly, we collect information necessary to evaluate your vehicle and provide quotation services. This includes:

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, and email address.
  • Vehicle specifics: Registration number, vehicle category, running condition, and location.
  • Optional communications: Notes, photos sent via WhatsApp, and pickup location details.

2. How Information is Used

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Calculate and provide an accurate vehicle scrap estimate.
  • Communicate via phone call or WhatsApp to discuss pickup logistics.
  • Facilitate legal vehicle scrapping documentation and owner record assistance.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

HSV Auto Recycling does NOT sell, rent, or lease customer contact information to commercial advertising companies or third-party marketing lists. We share data only when legally required by law enforcement or competent registering transport authorities (RTO) in relation to vehicle scrapping.
