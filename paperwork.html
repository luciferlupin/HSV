RTO Paperwork & RC De-Registration

Comprehensive documentation checklist and procedural guidance under Section 55 of the Motor Vehicles Act and MoRTH Vehicle Scrapping Rules.

Essential Verification Records

Document requirements vary depending on registration state, vehicle category, and hypothecation status. Standard documentation requested includes:

01

Original RC

The physical Registration Certificate (smart card or paper RC) proves legal vehicle title and records chassis/engine numbers.

02

Registered Owner KYC

Self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport of the individual whose identity appears on the VAHAN database.

03

PAN Card Verification

Verified tax identity required for compliance audits and transparent accounting of the scrap vehicle sale.

04

Bank Remittance Proof

A cancelled cheque or passbook copy matching the RC owner's name for direct digital transfer of scrap proceeds.

05

Lender NOC / Form 35

If the vehicle was purchased under loan/hypothecation, a No Objection Certificate from the financier is mandatory.

06

Chassis Stamping Trace

Physical pencil/carbon tracing of the vehicle chassis number on the application form for RTO verification.

CHASSIS DESTRUCTION & VAHAN DE-REGISTRATION

Understanding the legal lifecycle of your registration certificate after decommissioning.

When an End-of-Life Vehicle (ELV) is processed, it is permanently depolluted and dismantled so it can never re-enter the active fleet. Under Section 55 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the vehicle owner is legally obliged to notify the respective registering authority to record the vehicle's permanent scrapping.

HSV Auto Recycling coordinates the excision and photographic preservation of the chassis stamping, providing owners with certified dismantling records for filing the RC cancellation on Parivahan VAHAN.

Official Scrapping Certificate, RTO Cancellation & Chassis Cutout Protocol
Benefits of Proper RC Cancellation:
  • Eliminates future road tax and penalty liabilities.
  • Protects the owner from police inquiries if parts or numbers are misused elsewhere.
  • Official closure in the national vehicle registry (VAHAN).
