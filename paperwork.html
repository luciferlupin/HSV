● TRANSPARENT DOCUMENTATION

Paperwork shouldn't be confusing.

We explain what is required before the vehicle moves. HSV Auto Recycling guides vehicle owners through applicable government policy and norms.

Commonly Requested Documents

Document requirements vary depending on vehicle category, ownership structure, and registering authority jurisdiction. Documents commonly requested may include:

01

Original RC

The physical Registration Certificate (smart card or paper RC) proves legal ownership and registers vehicle specifics.

02

Owner ID Proof

Self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport of the individual whose name appears on the RC.

03

PAN Card

Copy of PAN card for owner identity verification and accounting documentation of the vehicle sale.

04

Bank Account Proof

A cancelled cheque or passbook copy matching the RC owner's name for direct digital transfer of scrap payment.

05

Bank NOC (If Financed)

If the vehicle was purchased under loan/hypothecation, a No Objection Certificate and Form 35 from the lender is needed.

06

Chassis Pencil Print

A physical carbon/pencil tracing of the vehicle chassis number on the application form for RTO verification.

ESSENTIAL KNOWLEDGE

WHAT HAPPENS TO MY RC?

Understanding the legal lifecycle of your registration certificate after scrapping.

When a vehicle is scrapped, it is permanently taken off the road so it can never be driven or resold. Under the Motor Vehicles Act (Section 55), the registered owner is required to inform the registering authority within whose jurisdiction the vehicle was registered.

HSV Auto Recycling assists the owner in collecting the necessary physical evidence (such as the chassis plate cutout and vehicle dismantling record) so that the RC cancellation can be processed according to applicable government policy and norms.

Benefits of Proper RC Cancellation:

  • Protects the owner from police inquiries if parts or numbers are misused elsewhere.
  • Official closure in the national vehicle registry (VAHAN).
