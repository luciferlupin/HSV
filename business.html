Commercial & Multi-Vehicle Enquiries

Managing outdated company fleets, transport vehicles, or yard inventories requires dependable communication. We discuss disposal logistics for:

Fleet Owners & Transporters

Commercial vans, light delivery trucks, and logistics vehicles that have reached end-of-fitness or registration expiry.

Automotive Dealers & Workshops

Trade-in vehicles that are not economically viable for resale, or abandoned customer vehicles with clear legal title.

Educational & Corporate Institutions

School buses, staff shuttles, and organizational vehicles requiring decommission documentation.

Insurance-Related Enquiries

Accident-damaged, total-loss salvage vehicles requiring lawful dismantling and disposal documentation.
